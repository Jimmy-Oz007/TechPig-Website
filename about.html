What is TechPig?

The Idea

TechPig is a technology learning project built around one simple challenge:

Build 100 IT projects.

Instead of only watching tutorials or reading about technology, the goal is to learn by actually building things.

The 100 Projects Challenge

The projects cover different areas of technology including web development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, hardware, cloud computing, game development and automation.

Some projects will succeed. Some will fail. Both are part of the process.

The YouTube Journey

TechPig documents the process through YouTube videos.

The goal is not to pretend everything works perfectly. It is to show the building, troubleshooting, mistakes, improvements and lessons behind each project.

Why TechPig?

Because getting better at technology requires building. TechPig is a public record of that process.
